One guide per part of the app: what it is for and how to use it.

- [The World in 3D](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/the-world/): walk the world, place and move NPCs and objects, draw their paths, and tie them to your quest.
- [Projects and the Quests dock](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/projects-and-canvas/): lay out quest chains beside the World, bring in existing quests and save your work.
- [Merging projects](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/merge-projects/): bring another project file's work into yours, with a preview of what the result keeps and drops.
- [Quest details, objectives and rewards](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/quest-details-and-objectives/): the quest editor and its modules.
- [Quest givers and enders](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/givers-and-enders/): who offers the quest and who takes it back.
- [Quest scripting](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/quest-scripting/): what NPCs, objects and areas do around a quest.
- [Spawn groups](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/spawn-groups/): only some of a set of spawns up at a time, with chances, as the Time-Lost Proto-Drake uses.
- [Quest rotations](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/quest-rotations/): daily and weekly quests that take turns, a few offered at each reset.
- [NPCs and objects](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/npcs-and-objects/): make new ones, change existing ones and choose how they look.
- [Readable objects](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/readable-objects/): books, notes and plaques with pages of text.
- [Items](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/items/): quest items and rewards.
- [Loot](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/loot/): what NPCs drop and chests hold.
- [Combat wizard](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/combat-wizard/): design how an NPC fights.
- [Patrols](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/patrols/): routes new NPCs walk, drawn in the World, and what they do along the way.
- [Test in game](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/test-in-game/): the commands to try a quest on your test server.
- [Exporting and applying](/azeroth-world-editor/guides/export-and-apply/): turn your work into SQL, for a quest or for the world changes.
